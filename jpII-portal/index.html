Ethiopian TVET context

A framework for practical learning

Occupational standards

Training and assessment should map to the applicable occupational standard and units of competence, with the standard’s version retained in each record.

Qualification pathways

Level and entry requirements belong to the approved occupation and qualification. Short courses and private lessons should not be presented as national qualifications without approval.

Workplace learning

Keep enterprise placement, trainer coordination, practical evidence, and supervisor verification connected through the learning journey.

Policy references

These references guide the portal structure. The college must confirm current regional directives, approved qualifications, and its own assessment, attendance, fee, and appeal procedures before production use.

  • Ministry of Justice: TVET Proclamation No. 954/2016
  • Regional assessment authority: national assessment directive
